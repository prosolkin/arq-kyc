    Proposed target design

    A proposal for discussion with Engineering. ARQ's current architecture is not known from the extracts, and parts of this may already exist. The order follows the roadmap: Phase 0 changes are small; the orchestration layer, registry checks and a second vendor are Phase 3, multi-quarter work.

    Requirements

    • Capture ID and selfie with quality checks on the phone.
    • Verify through one or more vendors, plus registries where available.
    • Decide with versioned per-country rules: approve, retake, review or reject, always with a reason code.
    • Never turn an execution error into a final rejection.
    • Emit the Phase 0 events; keep an audit trail of checks, rule versions and reviewers.
    • Protect identity data: encryption, access logs, and retention per local law, to confirm with Legal.

    Scale

    About — decisions on an average day in 2023 and — at most. Throughput is not the hard part. The hard parts are correct results when a vendor fails, fraud bursts, and data protection.

    Questions people ask

    Did the start-to-finish completion rate fall?

    These extracts cannot measure it. Approval among recorded decisions fell; completion needs start events and an observation window.

    Does KYC reject genuine customers?

    Probably some, but the extract cannot prove it. Execution-error rejections and liveness-spike failures are the strongest candidates; neither group's eligibility or outcome on retry is known.

    Did fraud go up?

    Fraud signals went up, mostly in two clusters. Signals are not adjudicated fraud; Phase 0 adjudicates a sample.

    Is the vendor at fault?

    The vendor's status page documents liveness incidents on Sep 1 and 6, 2023, on its US data centre. Whether ARQ was affected, and who caused the Sep 1–2 execution errors, is not established.

    How fast can it be fixed?

    Phase 0 takes about two weeks. Experiments follow in weeks 2–6, then wider rollout in weeks 6–13, and platform work over the next two quarters.

    What data is in this dashboard?

    Aggregated counts and rates only. No user ids, birth dates, images or record-level fields.
